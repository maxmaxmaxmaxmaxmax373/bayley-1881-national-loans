# The National Loans of the United States

**Rafael A. Bayley** · U.S. Treasury Department · 1881

A digital MyST Markdown edition of Bayley's 1881 report, prepared for the Tenth Census
of the United States. Covers the public debt from Independence through 1880, organized
by loan, with historical narrative and summary tables.

## About this edition

- **Source**: [Internet Archive](http://www.archive.org/details/cu31924030228245) (digitized from Cornell University Library)
- **Conversion**: PDF → MyST Markdown via `marker-pdf` + LLM-assisted cleanup
